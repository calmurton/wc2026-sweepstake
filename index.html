How It Works

Rules & tiebreakers

Winner & Second Place: Whoever drew the team that wins / is runner-up at the final on Sun 19 July.

Golden Boot Team: Whichever team the tournament's top individual goalscorer plays for. If two players tie on goals, the official FIFA tiebreakers apply (assists, then fewer minutes played).

Heaviest Defeat: The team that lost a single match by the biggest margin. Tiebreaker: most goals conceded in that match.

Wooden Spoon: Worst group-stage performance — fewest points, then worst goal difference, then fewest goals scored.

Early indicators on the prize board: Before there's enough data to crown a live leader, the Winner, Second Place, Golden Boot and Wooden Spoon lanes show the bookmaker favourite — marked with a blue "Odds favourite" pill. Once the tournament produces a calculated leader from match results, that lane switches to the green "Live leader" pill. Heaviest Defeat has no bookmaker market — it stays blank until the first result that produces a meaningful margin.
