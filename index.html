Rollover
If everyone is eliminated in the same gameweek, the pot carries over to a new round. Re-entry costs £10 for returning players, £20 for new entrants.
How It Works
1
Pick one team to win each gameweek
Before every gameweek deadline, submit your pick — one Premier League team you think will win. If your team wins, you survive and move on. If they draw or lose, you're out.
2
No repeat picks within a round
You can't pick the same team twice in the same round. Choose wisely — once you've used Arsenal, they're gone for the rest of the round. Picks reset only if there's a rollover.
3
Submit before the deadline
Picks must be in before the first match of the gameweek kicks off. Miss the deadline and you're automatically eliminated. No late entries.
4
Rollover if everyone goes out together
If all remaining players are eliminated in the same gameweek, the pot rolls over. Everyone can re-enter — returning players pay £10, new players pay £20. Picks reset for the new round.
5
Last one standing takes the pot
The sole surviving player wins the full pot. If two players both survive to the final gameweek and then both go out, the pot is split equally.
